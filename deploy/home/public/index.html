Everything the office does, in one system

  • Students and families

    Each student's details, classes, medical notes and contacts. Brothers and sisters linked, so families are kept together.

  • Classes and timetable

    Classes, teachers and the weekly timetable. Move students between classes in a few clicks.

  • Registers

    Teachers take the register on their phone. See who's absent or late, and answer parents' holiday and absence requests.

  • Fees

    Class fees, invoices, payments and who is overdue. Send families a reminder on WhatsApp with the amount written in.

  • Messages

    Message parents and teachers, with read ticks. Phone notifications for new messages and homework.

  • Sabak and progress

    Homework, merits, incidents, exams and end-of-year reports, all kept with each student.

Three apps, one system

  • Admin

    For the office and the head, on a computer or a phone.

  • Teacher app

    Registers, sabak, merits and messages for their own classes only.

  • Parent app

    Their children's attendance, homework, fees and messages. Nothing about anyone else's children.

The teacher and parent apps install from the browser to the phone's home screen. Nothing to download from an app store.

Children's data, kept safe

  • Your own copy

    Each madrasah has its own separate database and encryption keys, at its own address.

  • Stored in the EU

    Hosted in France, with nightly backups. Built for UK GDPR.

  • Only what they need

    Every page checks who is asking: teachers see their classes, parents see their own children.
