Students & families

Every child, every parent and how they’re connected.

  • Online application formParents apply on your website. Applications arrive as a waiting list you can accept, place in a class or decline, with an admission fee if you charge one.
  • Student recordsContact details, medical notes, photos, attachments, class history and your own custom fields.
  • StatusesWaiting list, accepted, current, on hold, suspended, left and finished, each with dates.
  • Parents and contactsPrimary contacts, emergency contacts and who can see which child in the parent app.
  • SiblingsFound automatically by surname and address, then used for family fees and discounts.
  • Walk-home consentParents give consent in the app; teachers see it by class.
  • Imports and exportsBring students and contacts in from a spreadsheet and download them any time.

Classes & timetables

Maktab, hifz and alim classes, on whatever days you run.

  • Classes with their own rulesAge group, main teacher, extra teachers with their own permissions, and fees per class or a school-wide default.
  • Weekly timetablesSessions by day with teacher, room and subject. Reuse a template across classes.
  • Year and age groupsSet the age range for each group and see where every child fits.
  • Moving upA new-year wizard moves classes, timings and students into the next academic year.
  • Class lists as PDFsPrintable summaries for one class or all of them.

Registers & attendance

Taken in a minute, understood at a glance.

  • Daily registers on any devicePresent, late, absent, left early, authorised or unauthorised. Use simple marks or DfE attendance codes.
  • Parent notices“Absent today” and “running late” from the parent app, shown on the register and the office dashboard.
  • Holiday requestsAccept as authorised or unauthorised, decline or ask for more information.
  • Attendance recordsEach child’s month-by-month record, minutes late and missed, with charts.
  • AlertsEmails when a child’s attendance drops below the level you set.
  • Absence calendar and statsBy class, by month or any date range, with CSV downloads.
  • Printable registersBlank or filled-in, by class and month.

Teachers & staff

Who works here, when, and what they can see.

  • Sign in and outOn a kiosk with a PIN, in the teacher app, or logged by the office. Weekly summaries and an absence calendar.
  • Teacher recordsContact details, DBS number and expiry, emergency contacts and working hours.
  • Office staff and access levelsGive each person only the access they need, from full control to fees only.
  • Class privilegesChoose what each teacher can do per class: registers, homework, merits, messages, reports.

Fees

Know who has paid, without a receipt book.

  • Fee structures that make the invoicesSet fees per class: monthly, termly or per number of sessions, with due dates. Invoices are created through the year for you.
  • Payments and receiptsRecord cash, card or bank transfer payments and email a receipt.
  • RemindersOverdue, upcoming and active invoices, with a reminder email you can edit.
  • Sibling discounts and adjustmentsA discount for the second and third child, or a standing change for any family.
  • ContributionsOne-off charges for trips, books or the building fund, invoiced to the families you choose.
  • ReportsMonthly and annual totals by invoice, student or family, with CSV downloads.
  • Bank transfer referencesA clear payment reference for each family so transfers are easy to match.

Messages & newsletters

One place for every conversation with families.

  • Messages with rulesParents, teachers and the office message each other only along the routes you allow. Lock a thread when it’s done.
  • NewslettersEmail parents, a class, teachers or the waiting list, using your templates.
  • Text messagesSend texts to groups or classes, with templates for absences, homework and fees.
  • Templates you can editEvery email and letter the system sends, in your own words.

Homework, exams & reports

Progress you can show parents.

  • End-of-year reportsExam results, attendance and homework pulled in for each child, teacher comments, and paragraph templates for behaviour, progress and effort. Generated as PDFs.
  • HomeworkFor a class or chosen children, with deadlines, uploads and reminders.
  • Exams and progress testsPapers per subject, marks entry and your own grade boundaries.
  • SubjectsQuran, Tajweed, Arabic, Fiqh, Seerah or your own, shared across timetables and reports.
  • ForumsClass discussion spaces for teachers and parents.

Merits & certificates

Recognise effort, not just results.

  • Merits and achievementsAward points, set milestones with bronze, silver and gold badges, and show them in the parent app.
  • CertificatesUpload your certificate design once and print a named one for any child.
  • Houses and competitionsGroup classes or students into teams and run competitions between them.
  • School shopSell books and uniform, or let children spend their merits.

Events & trips

Opt-ins and consent without the paper slips.

  • Events parents opt intoDates, timeslots, deadlines, terms and consent items. An invoice is raised automatically when a parent opts in.
  • School calendar and holidaysShown in each app, with a newsletter to tell families.
  • Calendar feedSubscribe in any calendar app.

Safeguarding & safety

Records you’ll be glad you kept.

  • Fire drillHold one button for two seconds and every class gets a live roll call from tonight’s registers and sign-ins. Every drill is saved.
  • IncidentsFor students, teachers and staff, with severity, action taken, and the option to tell parents.
  • Health and safety reportsDownload incidents for any date range.
  • Medical notesVisible to the people who need them.

Parent, teacher and student apps

In the phone’s browser. Nothing to install from a store.

  • You decide what each app showsHide fees, reports, homework or any other section, and choose which details teachers can see.
  • Parent appAttendance, homework, fees, reports, events, merits and messages for every child.
  • Teacher appRegisters, homework, merits, incidents, report comments, sign-in and the fire drill.
  • Student appTheir own homework and messages, if you switch it on.
  • Hijri datesShown alongside the usual date, in English or Arabic.

Settings & security

Built to be trusted with children’s data.

  • UK GDPR compliantRole-based access, parents limited to their own children, and an audit log of important changes.
  • Nightly backupsRestore if something goes wrong.
  • Academic yearsSwitch between years and compare them.
  • Your brandingYour logo, school details and email sender name on everything.
  • Free data importWe move your existing records in for you.
